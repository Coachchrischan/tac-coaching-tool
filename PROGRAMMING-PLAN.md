## The delivery format (changed 2026-08-31)

Phase 1 is a **two-day full-body split**, not the old Lower/Upper/Full three-day:

- **Full Body A** (Tuesday class): squat + upper focus.
- **Full Body B** (Thursday class): RDL + lower focus.
- **Friday strength is on hold** by the club's decision; the Friday class slot
  keeps existing on the timetable but carries no strength programming while
  that holds.

## Phase 1: Strength-Hypertrophy, 9 weeks (14 Sept)

Three 3-week microcycles. The compounds stay the same all phase and wave;
accessories interchange at each microcycle boundary; **week 3 of each
microcycle ends on a challenge** (Day A: Sally push-up challenge, mechanical
drop set plank, deadhang).

**Main lifts** (Back/Front Squat on A, Romanian Deadlift on B; bench family on
A follows the same reps):

| Micro | Weeks | Wave | Loading |
|-------|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | 1-3 | 9 / 7 / 5 | 65% / 70% / 75%, RPE 7 to 8 |
| 2 | 4-6 | 9 / 7 / 5 | 67.5% / 72.5% / 77.5%, RPE 7.5 to 8.5 |
| 3 | 7-9 | 7 / 5 / 3 | 75% / 80% / 85%, RPE 8 to 9 |

**Bench progression** (A): tempo (30X1) in micro 1, paused in micro 2, straight
bench on percentages in micro 3. **Chin/pull-up progression** (A): EMOM volume
in micro 1, full-rest strength sets in micro 2, top-set triples with back-offs
in micro 3.

**Accessories**: swap each micro, ascending volume inside it (week 1 base,
weeks 2 and 3 plus two reps with RPE climbing 7 to 8).

There is **no deload inside the nine weeks** (the standalone Deload/Skills week
was removed by the club). Flagged as a fatigue watch-point; the primer weeks
are the freshest entry the block gets.

## Phase 2: Strength, 5 weeks (16 Nov)

Structure to be confirmed with the club. The old plan's 7-5-3 and 5-3-1 mini
waves were written for six weeks and no longer fit as-is. The end-of-block
retest in Christmas week is also to be reconfirmed.

## Christmas: two-week gap

Detraining window (flagged by the roundtable): consider a maintenance option or
a return-to-training ramp in the first January week.

## Phase 3: Unilateral muscle-endurance (4 weeks, new year)

- Week 1: unilateral testing to get everyone sorted (lots of newcomers expected).
- Predominantly single-limb exercises; lots of tempo, time under tension, asymmetric holds.
- Higher-rep muscle-endurance focus, addressing imbalances at the start of the year.

## Phase 4: Hypertrophy (12 weeks)

- Aesthetic-based goals focus. Main strength movements still retained.
- Then back into the strength-hypertrophy / strength cycle.

## Coverage / variety tracking (all blocks)

Two tracking layers run side by side:
1. **Movement pattern** (shoulder rotation, hinge, squat, push, pull, ...)
2. **Muscle groups**, basic anatomical level (gastrocs, soleus, quads, hamstrings, ...)

Plus **trunk categories** to tick off: lower abs, rectus abdominis, rotation,
anti-rotation, anti-flexion, anti-extension.

Known gap in Phase 1 as written: the **carry pattern appears nowhere** in the
nine weeks (the old plan carried farmers carries). Raised with Chris.
